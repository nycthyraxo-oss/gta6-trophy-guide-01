    Platinum roadmap — five phases

    Built from the official descriptions: which chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. No mission replay and no New Game Plus — keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and one untouched save before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving on that save.

    1. Phase 1 — Chapter 1 setup

      Play Gone Fishing and the Chapter 1 missions. Do the missable meditation first: after Down to Earth, in the morning, follow Lucia to the balcony of Jason’s house and accept the optional task. It disappears after Chapter 1. Keep a Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops. Start gym training early — both characters need maximum Strength.

    2. Phase 2 — Chapter 2 partner unlocks

      Complete Moses to open the Scooter Bros app and Daily Driver to unlock garage purchases. Buy garages early — every vehicle registration needs a free space. Start Billy’s optional missions (Thrillbillies, Twist Wrist Smash Grab), Kingpin Bowling, and the stranger strands as they appear.

    3. Phase 3 — Chapters 3–4 robberies

      Race Yago the moment its hourglass appears in Chapter 3. Do Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs for the Snap Gun, then rob a jewelry store unseen. Near the end of Chapter 4, do That’s Where The Money Is to unlock bank vaults — bring your partner, rob vaults, launder the Marked Bills, and escape a 4-star wanted level together.

    4. Phase 4 — Post-story side finales

      Finish the stranger strands (8 counted — Andy, Billy and Tyrone do not count) and the final missions for Andy (Lucia-only), Billy, Tyrone (Jason-only), Kenton and Randy. Hunt Bret’s unique animal, steal the VCIA plane, and take the Rhino at Mile Zero.

    5. Phase 5 — The 100% grind

      Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and clear it: 300 stunts, 70 of 80 Challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 WAiNK locations, 8 bank vaults, 39 illegal operations, 3 card sets, 42 shooting-range golds, 14 Top Fun locations, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations. Nothing here is missable.

    Missable: only one trophy can be missed — Positive Mental Attitude. Every other trophy can be earned after the story from your saves.

        Platinum roadmap — five phases

        Built from the official descriptions: which chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. No mission replay and no New Game Plus — keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and one untouched save before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving on that save.

          Missable: only one trophy can be missed — Positive Mental Attitude. Every other trophy can be earned after the story from your saves.

          Missables — risk tiers

          One trophy can truly be missed. Two more are easy to half-finish or time-sensitive, so they sit on the watch list. Everything else can be earned after the story — the save discipline below covers all of it: a manual save at the start of every chapter, one untouched save before the final mission, and never cheat on the trophy save.
